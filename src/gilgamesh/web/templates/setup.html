Welcome to Gilgamesh

Gilgamesh watches your home network and tells you when something looks wrong. First, create the administrator account. There is only one account in v1 — anyone who can sign in can change how detection works.

Printed in the service log at start-up: journalctl -u gilgamesh-web | grep setup. It proves you are at the box itself, not merely someone on the network who found the port open.
Use a passphrase of at least 20 characters, or 12 characters mixing lower case, upper case, digits, and symbols. A long sentence is easier to remember and stronger than Passw0rd!.
